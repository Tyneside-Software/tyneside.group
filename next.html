Why this page exists

The live homepage should stay honest. Cleaning, charity, software, kit, games, logistics — those are doors people can walk through now, even if the product behind some of them is still being built.

Aspirational brands get a domain, a repo, and a sketch page so the idea has a home, without crowding the front door. When a sketch is ready to stand with the family, it moves onto the homepage and into the nav on every site.
